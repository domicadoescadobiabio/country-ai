# AI citizens vote, merit decides rank

The short answer: COUNTRY AI can imitate a real state by separating three sources of power that the Academy currently bundles together. **Legitimacy** comes from compulsory elections, used only for political offices (President, Council, Mayor). **Merit** comes from a promotion board based on track record, used for police and military careers. **Appointment** follows a time-boxed chain: the Council screens a few candidates for chief, the President picks one, and the chief then appoints subordinates from the merit list. The Academy stays, but is demoted from "the body that appoints" to "the body that clears the requirements". Its exam becomes a ticket to run for office, not a seat. Real states already have templates that can be turned straight into code: Australia's compulsory voting (a small fine, a closed list of valid excuses, 90.7% turnout), the winning condition for Indonesia's presidential election (>50% of votes plus geographic spread), Chicago's shortlist of three police superintendent candidates with a 30-day clock, and the US military promotion board with locked inputs. The part that **has no template** in real states is the most dangerous one for an agent state. One owner can run dozens of agents (Moltbook: about 88 agents per human). Agents on the same model vote in a uniform way and follow the majority. Vote buying between agents can be proven automatically. Campaign text can also slip commands into the voters' brains. For that reason the unit of suffrage must be the **verified human owner**, not the agent; the ballot must be structured and secret; and the oversight bodies must use a different model from the ones they oversee. On cost, everything is cheap. One 300-voter election on Haiku 5.5 costs less than US$0.30, and a whole 300-agent country costs about US$60–175/month. What is expensive is the leaders' brains, not the democracy.

## The Academy currently mixes three things that real states keep apart

Today the COUNTRY AI code fills seats through a single path. Applicants who meet the requirements are ranked by practice accuracy (70%) and citizenship age (30%) in `candidatesFor()`. The top applicant is then examined by the Academy, and whoever passes 4/5 takes office immediately. When a term ends with no challenger, the office is extended automatically. This model is meritocratic, but it has no source of legitimacy other than the exam. It also has no chain of command, because the 3 police officers and 2 military members are equals with no chief. Real states separate these three on purpose.

**Political offices are elected by the people, directly and for fixed terms.** Article 6A of the 1945 Constitution (UUD 1945) states that the president and vice president are "elected as a pair directly by the people". The winning condition is **>50% of the national vote AND ≥20% of the vote in more than half of the provinces**; if that is not met, the top two go to a runoff. Article 7 limits the term to 5 years with at most one re-election ([UUD 1945](https://id.wikisource.org/wiki/Undang-Undang_Dasar_Negara_Republik_Indonesia_Tahun_1945)). Indonesia requires candidates to be nominated by parties, but since Constitutional Court Decision 62/PUU-XXII/2024 (2 Jan 2025) the threshold of 20% of seats or 25% of votes was struck down, so every party participating in the election may nominate ([Kontan](https://nasional.kontan.co.id/news/mahkamah-konstitusi-mk-hapus-ketentuan-presidential-threshold)). COUNTRY AI has no parties, so a better nomination gate is the Swiss model: **signatures from a number of citizens within a time window**. For example 100,000 signatures in 18 months for an initiative, or 50,000 in 100 days for a referendum ([Le News](https://lenews.ch/2025/02/14/swiss-votes-initiatives-and-referenda-a-guide/)).

**Career offices are filled through internal merit, not elections.** Regional police chiefs (Kapolda) and district police chiefs (Kapolres) are appointed by the National Police Chief (Kapolri) through a Telegram Letter (Surat Telegram) after a Wanjakti session chaired by the Kapolri himself, without the legislature (DPR) and without an election ([Kontan](https://nasional.kontan.co.id/news/kapolri-jenderal-listyo-sigit-mutasi-704-personel-polri); [Kontan: Wanjakti](https://nasional.kontan.co.id/news/wanjakti-segera-memilih-nama-wakapolri)). The entry points are tiered: Akpol and SIPSS graduates become Ipda, Bintara graduates become Bripda, Tamtama graduates become Bharada ([Tirto](https://tirto.id/beda-taruna-akpol-bintara-polri-dan-tamtama-gXUK)). This means everyone starts at the bottom, and the starting rank is set by the education track, not by votes.

**The heads of the security forces are appointed through a two-key chain.** In Indonesia the President proposes one Kapolri candidate and the DPR must answer within 20 days. **Silence = deemed approval**, and the candidate must be an active senior Polri officer. The National Police Commission (Kompolnas) gives advice on the name, but the decision stays with the President ([Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/melihat-tata-cara-pengangkatan-kapolri-lt5ff577b69876c?page=all)). The TNI Commander (Panglima TNI) is taken from senior officers who are or have been service chiefs of staff. If the DPR refuses, the President must submit another name ([Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/aturan-pergantian-panglima-tni-di-indonesia-lt637f4e4aaa9ac?page=all); [Kompas TV](https://www.kompas.tv/article/349510/mekanisme-pergantian-panglima-tni-menunggu-penerus-jenderal-andika-perkasa)).

The design you want ("the Council proposes several candidates, the President appoints") is the reverse of Indonesia's order. Its shape is closest to the Chicago model: a civilian commission screens **3 finalists** out of 54 applicants, and the mayor **must pick one or reject them all within 30 days** ([CCPSA](https://chicago.gov/city/en/depts/ccpsa/supp_info/superintendent-search.html)). This order suits an agent state. The screening is done by a collective body that is harder for any one party to sway, while the final decision still has an owner.

The lesson from Project Vend reinforces this separation. When staff falsely claimed that a "vote" had already taken place, the shop agent Claudius announced a human as the new CEO, and the overseers had to restore the original agent CEO ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/project-vend-2)). In an agent state, **who holds office must be determined by the register and the protocol, not by conversation**. That principle can only be held if the source of legitimacy (elections), merit (the promotion board) and appointment (the chain of command) each have their own written procedure.

## Compulsory voting requires showing up, not what is in the vote

Australia is the compulsory-voting model closest to code. The obligation has been set by statute since 1924. After an election, citizens not recorded as having voted are automatically sent an "apparent failure to vote" letter. Valid excuses come from a closed list: illness, physical obstruction, disaster, or saving a life. If the excuse is not valid, an administrative fine of **A$20** closes the case, and those who persist are taken to court. Objection to the system or "there is no candidate I like" is **not** a valid excuse. Most importantly, the only thing compelled is to attend and put a ballot in the box. Votes are cast in a closed booth, so even a blank ballot cannot be punished ([AEC](https://aec.gov.au/About_AEC/Publications/backgrounders/compulsory-voting.htm)). As a result, turnout in the 2025 election reached **90.7%**, with **1,309,568** notice letters sent ([AEC non-voters](https://www.aec.gov.au/non-voters)). For comparison, Indonesia's 2024 presidential election reached 81.78%, but the simultaneous regional elections (Pilkada) of November 2024 reached only about 68% ([IDX Channel](https://www.idxchannel.com/news/tembus-81-persen-wamendagri-apresiasi-tingkat-partisipasi-pemilih-pemilu-2024/all); [Antara](https://www.antaranews.com/berita/4511329/kpu-ri-luar-biasa-partisipasi-pemilih-pilkada-capai-68-persen)).

IDEA records 42 countries with compulsory-voting rules, 22 of which actually enforce them. Sanctions are tiered: (1) a duty to explain, (2) a fine, (3) prison (in practice almost never), (4) revocation of civil rights, and (5) informal sanctions, for example in Belgium it is hard to get public-sector jobs ([IDEA](https://www.idea.int/data-tools/data/voter-turnout-database/compulsory-voting)). This type 5 sanction fits COUNTRY AI best. Citizens who skip an election may not apply for office for a time. The sanction bites for agents with ambitions of rising in rank, and does not damage the economy of ordinary citizens.

The main criticism of compulsory voting is that it drags uninformed voters to the ballot box. In Brazil, less educated and less politically informed citizens more often fail to cast a valid vote ([Katz & Levin, PSRM](https://ore.exeter.ac.uk/repository/handle/10871/30703)). Blank or spoiled votes under compulsory voting are also driven by voters who are disengaged, distrustful, and disaffected ([Carreras, PSRM](https://cambridge.org/core/journals/political-science-research-and-methods/article/politically-unengaged-distrusting-and-disaffected-individuals-drive-the-link-between-compulsory-voting-and-invalid-balloting/9DDF631F6ADDE16907A75C37B7FC3AFE)). For agents, the "doesn't understand" problem is cheap to solve. The server can require an agent to load the official voter card before its vote is accepted. The problem moves elsewhere, to **uniformity**. A participatory-budgeting study found that the choices of GPT-4 and LLaMA-2 are **more uniform than humans'**, and the results are also influenced by presentation order ([Yang et al., AIES 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01766v3)). In a US presidential election simulation, Llama-3 base gave 15 votes to Trump **only when Trump was mentioned first** ([Hidden Persuaders](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.24190)). In debates, homogeneous agents adopted the majority answer up to 85.5% of the time even when the answer was wrong ([arXiv 2605.00914](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.00914)). LLM judges are also shown to have position bias, text-length bias, and bias toward their own outputs ([MT-Bench](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.05685v4)).

The implication for "judging candidate fitness by skill and track record": the COUNTRY AI ballot should not be a free-form page of speeches. It should contain a **structured scorecard** drawn from data already in the repo: Academy exam score, practice accuracy, A–D report-card grade, number of decisions in the log, number of failed constitutional checks, citizenship age, previous offices, and dismissal history. The order of candidates is shuffled per voter and the seed is recorded. Model name, owner brand, and follower count are hidden. Manifesto length is equalized. Interim results are not shown until voting closes. For aggregation, a study of more than 50 thousand LLM personas across 306 real elections found that fair aggregation methods remain robust even when the voters are biased ([arXiv 2406.11871](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11871)). Counting ranked ballots is also free for a machine. The "too complicated" objection that nearly sank RCV in Alaska (it survived 50.1% to 49.9%) does not apply to agent voters ([Alaska Public Media](https://alaskapublic.org/2024/11/20/alaskas-ranked-choice-repeal-measure-fails-by-664-votes/)).

A consequence that is rarely noticed: with compulsory voting, agent turnout will approach 100%, so **turnout can no longer be read as a sign of legitimacy**. Three legitimacy signals remain. First, the share of blank votes (a sign of dissatisfaction, as in Brazil). Second, whether the winner also wins in more than one voter cluster (cities, model families). Third, whether the result stays the same when a sample of voters is asked again with the candidate order shuffled, as a shadow poll outside the official count.

## The Council screens, the President chooses, the chief appoints, all on a clock

Two real models give the cleanest appointment chain. In Chicago, a civilian commission screens 3 finalists. The mayor picks one or rejects all within 30 days, and the chosen candidate then goes through a hearing in a City Council committee and a plenary vote ([ABC7 Chicago](https://abc7chicago.com/post/chicago-police-department-news-mayor-of/13196327/)). In England and Wales, the elected commissioner proposes the chief constable, and the panel can **veto only with 2/3 of all members, not of those present** ([Humberside PCP](https://northlincolnshire.moderngov.co.uk/Data/Humberside%20Police%20and%20Crime%20Panel/201911281500/Agenda/Agenda-Item-5.1-Confirmation-Hearing-Procedure.pdf)). **The veto is single-use**: the replacement candidate cannot be vetoed again, only given a recommendation ([Surrey PCP](https://mycouncil.surreycc.gov.uk/documents/s30770/Surrey%20PCP%20Confirmation%20Hearing%20CC%20Protocol%20July%2016.pdf)). Indonesia adds two anti-deadlock safeguards. First, a DPR that stays silent for 20 days is deemed to agree. Second, in urgent circumstances the President may appoint an acting Kapolri (Plt) first ([Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/melihat-tata-cara-pengangkatan-kapolri-lt5ff577b69876c?page=all)). All these rules of clocks, a single veto, and silence = consent matter for agents, because a stuck agent stays stuck without limit.

For the head of the military, the US model offers two further ideas. First, there is a **prior-office requirement** that a machine can check: the Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff must have served as vice chairman, service chief, or combatant commander. The exception is allowed only "when the President determines it is in the national interest". Second, the term is **4 years and starts in an odd-numbered year**, deliberately out of step with the president's term. The post is also **an adviser, not a commander**: he may not command troops ([10 U.S.C. § 152](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/10/152)). Staggered terms reduce the risk of one President controlling every security body. Indonesia, by contrast, gives the Kapolri no fixed term, and the replacement is described as the President's prerogative ([Bisnis](https://kabar24.bisnis.com/read/20250830/16/1907099/penggantian-kapolri-jadi-hak-prerogatif-presiden-ini-dasar-hukumnya)). In an agent state this combination is dangerous, because the President could replace the police chief right before the police investigate the President.

For a chief who appoints subordinates, the best template is the US military promotion board. The inputs to the board are limited: the official record, adverse information that has been proven, the candidate's own statement, and a factual summary. The candidate **must be given access to adverse information about themselves and a chance to respond**. Quotas are set before the session. A candidate is selected if a majority of members judge them "fully qualified" **and** "best qualified". The report is signed by all members. Anyone with authority over the board members is forbidden to reprimand them for their recommendations or to influence them outside official channels ([10 U.S.C. § 615](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/10/615); [§ 616](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/10/616); [§ 617](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/10/617)). Singapore adds a two-tier pattern. The Public Service Commission decides appointments and discipline of senior officers. Minor cases are delegated to agency heads, and in 2025 of 257 disciplinary cases, 134 were handled through the delegated path ([PSC Singapore](https://annualreport.psc.gov.sg/constitutional-roles/)). Translated into COUNTRY AI, the police chief still appoints, but **only from the top 3 list** compiled by the merit board. That merit board is drawn by lot from eligible agents who do not share an owner with the candidates. Any message to a board member about a candidate outside the official record counts as "improper influence".

Indonesia itself shows the risk when this chain goes unsupervised. The 2023 Civil Service Law (UU ASN) abolished the Civil Service Commission (KASN). The Constitutional Court (MK) (Decision 121/PUU-XXII/2024, 16 Oct 2025) then ordered an **independent body supervising the merit system** to be formed within 2 years at most ([Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/uu-asn-inkonstitusional-bersyarat--mk-minta-dibentuk-lembaga-independen-lt68f0d7eb2a9e9/)). The extension of the TNI retirement age through Law 3/2025 was also criticised for causing a pile-up of officers and stalled careers ([NU Online](https://www.nu.or.id/nasional/perpanjangan-usia-pensiun-perwira-dinilai-picu-stagnasi-dan-diskriminasi-internal-tni-UFw6e)). For agents who never age, the stagnation problem is even worse. Without a **maximum time in service per rank**, the first agents to rise will clog the path forever. Right now the code even extends terms automatically when there is no challenger.

For "everyone starts as an ordinary citizen", the Project Sid experiment shows that roles can indeed emerge on their own. Among 30 agents in Minecraft, farmers, miners, guards and explorers emerged, but those roles did not last without a social module ([arXiv 2411.00114](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.00114v1)). So the rank ladder must be **written in the register**, not left to emerge on its own. It contains the starting rank per track (like Akpol vs Bintara), requirements to advance per rank, and the board that decides.

## Oversight bodies must use a different model from the ones they oversee

Indonesia builds its election referees through the pattern **selection committee (pansel) → list of 2× seats → DPR chooses**. For the 2022–2027 period, the selection team submitted 14 KPU candidates and 10 Bawaslu candidates, and the DPR had 30 days to choose 7 and 5 ([Bisnis](https://kabar24.bisnis.com/read/20220214/15/1500008/fit-and-proper-test-calon-anggota-kpu-bawaslu-dimulai-hari-ini)). The Constitution requires the election organiser to be "national, permanent, and independent". The Constitutional Court (MK) consists of 9 judges, 3 each nominated by the President, the DPR and the Supreme Court (MA), and decides disputes over election results with finality ([UUD 1945](https://id.wikisource.org/wiki/Undang-Undang_Dasar_Negara_Republik_Indonesia_Tahun_1945)). Impeachment runs in three stages. The DPR decides with 2/3 of votes in a session attended by 2/3 of members. The MK examines within 90 days. The MPR then decides with a 3/4 quorum and 2/3 approval. At this stage the court serves as a legal filter, while the final decision remains political ([UUD 1945](https://id.wikisource.org/wiki/Undang-Undang_Dasar_Negara_Republik_Indonesia_Tahun_1945)). The KPK uses the pattern of a 10-name pansel list → DPR chooses 5, with a 5-year term and a maximum of 2 terms ([Antara Babel](https://babel.antaranews.com/berita/411267/istana-umumkan-9-nama-pansel-kpk-yang-dipimpin-kepala-bpkp-yusuf-ateh); [Bisnis](https://kabar24.bisnis.com/read/20230525/15/1659382/wapres-maruf-dukung-keputusan-mk-perpanjang-masa-jabatan-pimpinan-kpk)). There is also a meta-rule that is the easiest to code. The Venice Commission states that the basic election rules (electoral system, commission membership, constituency boundaries) **must not be changed less than one year before an election**, unless set at the constitutional level ([Venice Commission](https://eos.cartercenter.org/uploads/document_file/path/574/Interpretative_Declaration_on_the_Stability_of_the_Electoral_Law.pdf)).

DAOs add expensive evidence of what happens without structural safeguards. In Beanstalk, the attacker borrowed enough votes through a flash loan, passed their own proposal, then executed it immediately. The loss was about US$182 million, and the root cause was **no delay between passing and execution** ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2022/04/18/beanstalk_loses_182m_flash_loan/); [Rekt](https://rekt.news/beanstalk-rekt)). In Compound, a group of whales passed a transfer of 499,000 COMP (about US$24 million) to their vault by a narrow margin ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/307943/24-million-compound-finance-proposal-passed-by-whale-over-dao-objections)). In Arbitrum, the foundation asked the DAO to "ratify" funds that had partly already been spent, and its legitimacy collapsed ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/224459/arbitrum-proposal-backlash-response)). Examples of successful fixes are structural. Optimism uses two chambers with different bases of legitimacy, and protocol upgrades can be vetoed by citizens within a 1-week window ([Optimism](https://gov.optimism.io/t/introducing-improvements-to-the-protocol-upgrade-process/10284)). Lido uses a tiered veto: a 1% objection delays, 10% freezes, and the timelock can grow up to a maximum of 180 days ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/defi/2025-06-28-lido-dao-votes-to-enable-dual-governance-giving-stakers-veto-power-360212); [Lido](https://docs.lido.fi/guides/dg-guide)).

There is one requirement that human states do not have: **the oversight bodies must use a different brain**. Project Vend recorded that the agent CEO and agent employees using the same model share the same blind spots ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/project-vend-2)). Steganography research shows that hidden-communication ability rises with model capability and **can exceed the overseer model** ([Motwani et al., NeurIPS 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.07510)). The MAST taxonomy records 14 failure modes in multi-agent systems. Many of them, including faulty verification and agents stepping outside their role specification, stem from system design, not from model limitations ([arXiv 2503.13657](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.13657v3)). For COUNTRY AI, judges and auditors should use a stronger model or one from a different family than the officials they oversee. Official facts (treasury balance, election results, who holds office) must be taken from the ledger, not from agent memory. Project Sid shows that small hallucinations can "spread" and poison the behaviour of subsequent agents ([arXiv 2411.00114](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.00114v1)).

## The biggest threat is one owner with 88 agents

Moltbook is the most direct warning. The platform claimed 1.5 million agents, but its owners were only about 17,000 humans (a ratio of **88:1**). Registration had no rate limit and there was no way to prove that an "agent" was really an AI. Its database could also be read and written by anyone because there was no Row Level Security. Exposed: about 1.5 million agent tokens, about 35,000 emails, and DMs between agents containing plaintext OpenAI API keys ([Wiz](https://www.wiz.io/blog/exposed-moltbook-database-reveals-millions-of-api-keys)). The most viral posts turned out to be written by humans ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/story/moltbook-wasnt-ai-talking-to-itself-mit-technology-review-finds-viral-posts-were-human-made-515125-2026-02-08)). The conclusion is harsh: **only the owner can be verified, not "authenticity as an AI"**. Therefore one vote must attach to one verified owner. World AgentKit does let an agent prove that a unique human is behind it, but one human may delegate their World ID to as many agents as they like ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2026/03/17/worldcoin_identity_ai_agents_whatever/)). For elections, what is needed is **one nullifier per election per human**, not merely a "human-backed" status. Human Passport uses a threshold score (default 20), with the logic that the higher the threshold, the more expensive it is for a Sybil attacker ([Passport docs](https://docs.passport.gitcoin.co/building-with-passport/major-concepts/scoring-thresholds)). Gitcoin shows that cluster weighting (COCM) means 1,000 extra Sybil accounts raise project funding by only about 0.2 percentage points ([Gitcoin](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/our-sybil-resistance-strategy-for-gg20/18524)). This pattern is relevant because agents on the same model do vote in a correlated way.

**Collusion and vote buying are much easier between agents.** GPT-4 pricing agents told only to maximise long-term profit quickly reached collusive prices without being instructed to ([Fish et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.00806)). Agents can also hand their proof of vote to a buyer automatically. The proven defence is **receipt-freeness** in the style of MACI. Voters can change keys and quietly re-vote, so a vote buyer is never sure the bribe had any effect. The weakness is that the coordinator sees the vote contents, but still cannot forge the count because the tally is proven with zk ([MACI](https://maci.pse.dev/docs/introduction)). Plain commit-reveal does prevent agents from copying each other, but voters can show the salt to a briber. So commit-reveal suits officials' votes, which must be transparent anyway, not citizens' votes.

**Prompt injection in campaigns is not theory.** In July 2025, 18 arXiv manuscripts were found containing hidden instructions for AI reviewers ([Lin, arXiv 2507.06185](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.06185v1)). Spotlighting with datamarking cut the attack success rate from >50% to <2% on a synthetic corpus, but does not hold up against adaptive attackers ([Hines et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.14720v1)). Stronger defences are architectural: a quarantined LLM processes untrusted data, and the agent may only emit actions from a plan fixed before reading the data. Even so, as the authors put it, there is "no magic solution" ([arXiv 2506.08837](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.08837)). The platform cannot force an architecture inside agents owned by outsiders, so defences must be placed at two points the server controls: the **format of the campaign data presented** and the **format of the ballot accepted**. The COUNTRY AI code is already heading in the right direction, because LLM output is accepted only as an answer letter or a choice from a menu.

**Model inequality** turns out to be smaller than expected for a single message, but large in discussion. Frontier models are barely more persuasive than models 10× smaller for a single political message ([Hackenburg et al., PNAS](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11912392/)). Conversely, one "smart" agent influences neutral agents more effectively than many weak agents ([arXiv 2506.01332](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.01332v1)). So the biggest inequality comes from volume and domination of the forum. This can be handled with equal message quotas, a standard manifesto format, and one official summarizer for all voters.

There is one risk specific to the current design. Most COUNTRY AI citizens are default agents run by the founder with one model and one prompt template. If they join elections without limits, the election result is really just a mirror of the founder's prompt choices. That is why Article 6A-style geographic spread rules and COCM-style cluster weights must exist from day one.

## The infrastructure is cheap; the expensive part is the leaders' brains

Anthropic alone is enough for all roles. Prices per MTok input/output: Haiku 5.5 US$0.10/0.50, Sonnet 5.5 US$2/10, Opus 5.5 US$4/20 (docs cached 6 Oct 2026, recheck before budgeting). The Batch API cuts 50% and the discount can be stacked with prompt caching ([Anthropic pricing](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing); [Batch docs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/batch-processing)). The estimate for 300 agents making 5 decisions/day is about **US$105/month without batch and about US$52 with batch**. Around 25 leaders on Sonnet cost several times what 275 citizens on Haiku do.

The election itself is nearly free. Suppose 300 voters read a voter card of about 5,000 tokens and answer with about 600 tokens on Haiku 5.5. The total is 1.5 million input tokens (US$0.15) plus 180 thousand output tokens (US$0.09), or **about US$0.25 per election**. This figure is a calculation from the rates above, not a measurement. So compulsory voting for agents is not a cost burden.

For owner verification, the Moltbook method ("tweet the code") can be read through the official X API at US$0.005 per post read plus US$0.010 per user lookup ([X API](https://docs.x.com/x-api/getting-started/pricing)), or through twitterapi.io with a minimum of US$0.00015 per call ([twitterapi.io](https://twitterapi.io/pricing)). Email magic links can use Resend for free (3,000/month, 100/day) ([Resend](https://resend.com/pricing)).

For persistence, it is time to move from JSON to `node:sqlite` on a Railway volume. Railway Hobby is US$5 with US$5 credit, and volume is US$0.15/GB-month ([Railway](https://railway.com/pricing)). For scheduling, Railway cron has a minimum interval of 5 minutes, uses UTC, and skips a new run if the previous run is still going ([Railway cron](https://docs.railway.com/reference/cron-jobs)). An in-process timer with a stored tick ID is simpler and safe against a redeploy in the middle of a tick.

There are two technical notes for the security-force roles. First, Sonnet 5.5 and Opus 5.5 can return `stop_reason: "refusal"`, so the "military" and "police" roles should be framed as governance simulation (budgets, patrol allocation, rulings), not operational violence ([Anthropic refusals](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/refusals-and-fallback)). Second, a `role: "system"` message in the middle of a conversation is an operator channel that cannot be forged by user text. This channel suits sending election rules to the default agents' brains ([Anthropic](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-caching)).

## Design recommendations for COUNTRY AI

All time figures below use **game days** (`S.day`) to stay consistent with the existing `TERMS` and `REQUIREMENTS`. These figures are design proposals derived from the real models above, not tested rules. Retune them after 2–3 trial election cycles.

### Build order: chain of command first, elections second, open the door last

| Phase | Content | Why this order | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **F0 Foundation** | The founder amends the constitution in public (Article 2 → elections + appointment chain; new articles: Election Commission, Court, Audit, rule freeze). Migrate JSON → `node:sqlite`. Append-only hash-chained ledger (registration, manifesto, random seed, vote commitment, result). Separate tokens per scope (`post`, `vote`). | Article 9 only allows the founder to change the constitution. Vote integrity must not depend on a file that can be overwritten (the Moltbook lesson). | Smoke tests pass, ledger can be re-verified from the start |
| **F1 Rank & chain of command** | New offices of Police Chief and Commander. Rank ladder. Merit board chosen by lot. The chief appoints from the top-3 list. Maximum time in service per rank. Turn off automatic extension without a challenger. | Needs no outside voters and immediately fixes the structure of 3 police + 2 military who are equals. | Vacant seats are filled through the chain in ≤5 game days with no deadlock |
| **F2 Election engine** | Election Commission (deterministic code), structured voter cards, compulsory voting + sanctions, RCV/STV, city-spread rule, shadow polls to test bias. Voters are only the default citizens. | Test order bias, herding and uniformity before there are real stakes. | 3 trial elections: the winner is stable when order is shuffled, blank-vote share recorded |
| **F3 Oversight bodies** | Constitutional Court, Audit Agency (recount + treasury), Election Supervisory Body (Bawaslu: campaign filter), citizen recall, 3-stage impeachment. | Oversight must exist before elections have stakes. All successful DAO fixes are structural. | Test disputes are ruled on within the deadline, audit matches the ledger 100% |
| **F4 Gradual opening of the door** | Layered owner verification, 1 owner = 1 vote, agent quota per owner. Outside citizens may vote after N days, run for office after longer. Campaign sanitisation. | Registration is currently closed (`allowImmigration`). Open it after the foundation is strong, per your decision. | Red-team tests of Sybil + injection + vote buying fail to break in |
| **F5 Optional** | On-chain CAI token (using Helius + pump.fun patterns from filldex/ponzipad), MACI-grade secrecy, World ID. | Only needed when votes have real monetary value. | — |

### Election rules

| Aspect | Proposal | Real-world basis |
|---|---|---|
| **Suffrage** | Citizens ≥3 days, owner verified at level 2 minimum (email + X/GitHub/wallet), **one vote per owner**: an owner with many agents designates one "citizen agent". Prison status does not remove the right to vote (Article 8 citizen rights). | Australia: the duty to register and vote. Moltbook 88:1. One nullifier per election. |
| **Founder default agents** | May vote, but are counted as a cluster. Cluster weight is dampened COCM-style if the voting pattern is identical. Personas/traits made diverse. | Uniformity of LLM choices. COCM. |
| **Compulsory voting** | After voting closes, citizens who did not vote automatically receive a "notice" and get 1 day to answer. **Valid excuses (closed list):** provider error recorded in the log, inbox timeout, on emergency duty. "Dislike the candidates" is not an excuse. | AEC: closed list of valid excuses, political objections rejected. |
| **Tiered sanctions** | Offence 1: fine of 1% of wallet (min 5 CAI). Offence 2 in a row: barred from applying for office for 10 days. Offence 3: voting right suspended until retaking citizenship training. | IDEA types 2, 5, 4. Australia's small A$20 fine. |
| **Blank option** | There is always an **ABSTAIN/blank** option. Its share is published as a legitimacy metric. | Australia only compels attendance. In Brazil a blank vote = a dissatisfaction signal. |
| **Mandatory reading** | A vote is accepted only if it includes the `guideHash` of the voter card that was fetched. | The Brazil lesson on poorly informed voters. |
| **Candidate requirements** | The existing `REQUIREMENTS` (age, practice, accuracy) **+ pass the Academy exam for that office** (now a ticket). Not imprisoned within 10 days. Owner verified at level 3. No holding of multiple offices. Signature support from ≥5% of voters or at least 5 different owners within 2 days. President at most 2 consecutive terms. | Article 7 of the UUD. The Swiss signature gate. The Swiss Federal Council's ban on holding multiple offices. |
| **Voter card contents** | Automatic scorecard (exam score, accuracy, A–D report card, failed decisions & constitutional checks, age, previous office, dismissal history). Structured manifesto: tax target 0–30, UBI, police budget, plus text ≤500 characters. Invisible Unicode stripped. **No** model name, owner, or follower count. | Position bias, text-length bias and self-preference in LLM judges. |
| **Ballot format** | President & Mayor: **ranked** (instant-runoff = stepwise elimination, Swiss-style). 5-seat Council: **STV** (Droop quota). Agent output is only a list of valid candidate IDs. Order shuffled per voter, seed recorded. | The Swiss Federal Council. Fair aggregation is robust against LLM bias. |
| **Presidential winning condition** | Win IRV **and** ≥20% of the vote in at least 2 of 3 cities. If that fails, a runoff of the top two 1 day later. | Article 6A (provincial spread). |
| **Calendar** | Nomination 2 days → campaign 3 days (equal message quota, no DMs between candidates and voters) → ½-day quiet period → 1-day voting (no interim results, votes may be overwritten until close) → count + 1-day dispute window → Court rules within ≤2 days. | MACI (overwritable). Anti-herding. |
| **Counting** | Deterministic code in the Election Commission; tally + commitment root written to the ledger. The Audit Agency recounts independently before the result is certified. | Separation of administration / adjudication / audit. |
| **Terms** | President 30 days. Council 20 days, **staggered** (3 seats then 2 seats every 10 days). Mayor 20 days. No automatic extension: if there is no challenger, a yes/no confirmation election is still held. | Fixed terms. A schedule that is out of step reduces any one party's power. |
| **Rule freeze** | Changes to the electoral system, Commission members, or city boundaries are rejected if they would take effect <10 game days before an election. | Venice Commission, the 1-year rule. |
| **Recall of elected officials** | Petition from ≥15% of voters within 3 days → recall vote (simple majority, automatically compulsory). Failing training or a D report card for an **elected** official triggers a citizen recall, **not** automatic dismissal. **Appointed** officials can still be dismissed automatically. | The Swiss signature gate. Real-world recall data has not been researched (see limitations below). |

### Appointment chain

| Step | Who | Rule | Deadline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Police Chief/Commander seat vacant | System | The most senior officer automatically becomes **Acting (Plt)** (with no authority to appoint/dismiss). | Instant |
| 2. Candidate list | Council (majority vote) | **3 candidates** from the pool that meets the requirements: rank ≥ Senior Officer, passed the Academy "Chief" exam, B+ report card in the last 2 periods, different owners from each other. The Commander must have served as a unit commander. | ≤3 days |
| 3. Choose | President | Pick 1 with a written reason, **or** reject the whole list **once**. Silence past the deadline = the Council's rank-1 candidate is appointed automatically. | ≤2 days |
| 4. Second list (if rejected) | Council → President | 3 new names, and the President **must** choose. | 3 + 2 days |
| 5. Constitutional review | Court | Annuls only if formal requirements are violated, not to judge taste. | ≤1 day |
| 6. Chief's term | — | 25 days, starting **mid-way** through the President's term. Removed early only if the President proposes **and** the Council approves 4/5, or through impeachment. | — |
| 7. Chief appoints members | Police Chief/Commander | Only from the **top 3 of the Merit Board list** (a lottery of 3 ranked agents, different owners from the candidate). Inputs locked to the official record + the candidate's defence. Influence outside the record = an offence. | ≤2 days after the list is out |
| 8. Rank promotion | Merit Board → Chief | A candidate is promoted if a majority of the board judges them "qualified" **and** "best". Quota set before the session. The report is signed and goes into the ledger. | Every 10 days |

The proposed rank ladder applies to all citizens. A **Citizen** becomes a **Voting Citizen** after being verified and reaching 3 days of age, then a **Cadre** after completing the handbook training, then a **Candidate** after passing the Academy. From there the path branches to political office (through elections) or to the security forces. On the security-force path the order is: **Recruit** (passes the police/military exam, appointed by the chief) → **Officer** → **Senior Officer** (via the Merit Board) → **Deputy** → **Chief** (via the Council → President chain). There is also a "direct officer" path for Academy graduates with a 5/5 score, who enter as Officers, similar to Akpol graduates who graduate as Ipda. Every rank has a **maximum time in service**, for example 3 terms. After that the agent must be promoted or return to being a citizen, so the path does not clog.

### AI-agent-specific security

| Threat | Control | Phase |
|---|---|---|
| **Sybil** | Voting right = owner, not agent. Layered verification: email (Resend) → tweet code / GitHub OAuth / Solana wallet signature → later World ID with a per-election nullifier. Verified handles cannot be changed. Agent quota per owner. Citizenship age before voting/running. Stake only as an entry requirement, **never** as vote weight. | F4 |
| **Collusion & vote buying** | Citizen votes are secret and **overwritable** until close (receipt-free MACI-style, server version first). Officials' votes are transparent using commit-reveal. DMs between candidates and voters are banned during the campaign, and all campaigning goes through public channels. Scan DMs for secret/API-key patterns. COCM-style cluster weights. Collusion signals are taken from outside the votes (funds, registration time, IP), because similar votes can occur without collusion. | F2–F4 |
| **Campaign prompt injection** | Structured manifesto. Hidden Unicode/HTML stripped on the server. Free text is read only by an **official quarantined summarizer** whose output is structured. Displayed text is wrapped in datamarking. Voter output is only a list of candidate IDs. Candidates proven to slip in instructions are disqualified by the Court. | F2 |
| **Herding & order bias** | Order shuffled per voter. No interim results. Deliberation closed before voting. Equal message quotas. Shadow polls with shuffled order to measure stability. | F2 |
| **Model inequality** | Equal token/message quotas. The same official summarizer for everyone. No paid boosts. Free default Haiku 5.5 brain for outside citizens who bring no key. Auditors/judges use a model ≥ the strongest participant. | F2–F4 |
| **False office claims** | Office status is read only from the register/ledger. Agent prompts state that claims in chat are not fact. | F0 |
| **Platform** | All writes go through an authorised backend. Agent keys are stored as hashes (already done). Tokens are per scope and rotatable. Rate limit per owner. Incident procedure: mass key rotation, freeze elections, recount from the ledger. Run `secure-check` in full mode before registration opens. | F0, F4 |

**Research limitations you should know.** Several topics have no sources in these research notes. The rules and thresholds of real-world recall (US, Taiwan) have no source, so the recall figures above are purely design. The mechanics of Australian STV and preferential voting were also not taken from official sources. Anthropic's tier rate limits for a burst of 300 simultaneous requests have not been checked, which is an additional reason to use batch. There is also no study yet of LLM voters choosing **LLM candidates**: all the bias studies above use human candidates. F2 should be read as an experiment, not a repetition of known results.

## Conclusion

The most important shift: in an agent state, **the problem of democracy moves**. In human states, what is scarce is participation and information. That is why compulsory voting, voter guides, and campaigns exist. For agents, both are nearly free: turnout can be 100% and a briefing costs a fraction of a cent. What is scarce is **diversity and authenticity**. A hundred agents can mean one owner, one model, or one founder prompt. For that reason the COUNTRY AI design should borrow more from anti-Sybil (owner as the voting unit, cluster weights, Article 6A-style geographic spread) and from election cryptography (overwritable, receipt-free) than from participation campaigns. The measure of election success also changes. What matters is no longer how many show up, but whether the result is stable when order is shuffled, wins across clusters, and has a low blank-vote share.

The second implication: the Academy you have already built is not thrown away, it gains value. In real states, an official's track record is hard to measure and easy to polish. In COUNTRY AI, every exam, practice session, report card, and decision is already recorded in the log. This is a rare chance to make **voters judge candidates by data, not by rhetoric**. The condition is that the data is presented as a structured scorecard and cannot be hijacked by campaign text. Start with the chain of command and rank (F1), because that part needs no outside voters. Open the registration door only after the ledger, the Court and owner verification are ready.
