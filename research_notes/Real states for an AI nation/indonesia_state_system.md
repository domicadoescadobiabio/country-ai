# Indonesia: how the state elects & appoints government leaders, police, military (a model for an AI-agent state)

Research notes as of 10 Nov 2026. Focus: rules that can be translated into simulation rules. Web search results contained many old articles; dates of rulings/laws are noted explicitly. Claims without a verified source were moved to "Gaps".

## 1. Presidential election, legislative elections (DPR/DPD/DPRD) and regional elections (Pilkada): who may run, thresholds, how to vote, terms of office

### Takeaway
The president + vice president are directly elected as one ticket, nominated ONLY by parties/coalitions of parties participating in the election; since Constitutional Court (MK) Decision 62/PUU-XXII/2024 (2 Jan 2025) the 20% seats/25% votes threshold was struck down, so every party participating in the election may nominate. Winning = >50% of the national vote + ≥20% of the vote in more than half of the provinces; if not met → a runoff of the top two. The term is 5 years, with a maximum of 1 re-election (2 terms). The DPR (House of Representatives) is elected by open-list proportional representation (Sainte-Laguë) with a 4% parliamentary threshold (last applied for 2024). From the 2029 cycle, national and local elections are separated (MK 135, 26 Jun 2025).

### Cited Findings
**Presidential election (constitution)**
- Article 6A of the 1945 Constitution (UUD 1945): the president & vice president are "elected as a pair directly by the people"; the pair is "nominated by a political party or a coalition of political parties" participating in the election before the election; inaugurated if they obtain >50% of the national vote AND ≥20% of the vote in each province spread across more than half of the provinces; if none, the top two pairs go to a further round chosen directly — [UUD 1945, Wikisource](https://id.wikisource.org/wiki/Undang-Undang_Dasar_Negara_Republik_Indonesia_Tahun_1945)
- Article 7 UUD: a term of 5 years, after which one may be re-elected to the same office for only one further term — [UUD 1945](https://id.wikisource.org/wiki/Undang-Undang_Dasar_Negara_Republik_Indonesia_Tahun_1945)
- Article 7C: the president cannot freeze/dissolve the DPR — [UUD 1945](https://id.wikisource.org/wiki/Undang-Undang_Dasar_Negara_Republik_Indonesia_Tahun_1945)
- There are no independent presidential candidates: the nomination route is only via parties (Article 6A paragraph 2) — [UUD 1945](https://id.wikisource.org/wiki/Undang-Undang_Dasar_Negara_Republik_Indonesia_Tahun_1945)

**Presidential nomination threshold (presidential threshold)**
- Article 222 of Law 7/2017 formerly required a party/coalition to hold ≥20% of DPR seats or ≥25% of valid national votes in the previous DPR election — [Kontan](https://nasional.kontan.co.id/news/mahkamah-konstitusi-mk-hapus-ketentuan-presidential-threshold); [Wikipedia: Indonesian electoral law of 2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_electoral_law_of_2017)
- MK Decision 62/PUU-XXII/2024, read on Thursday 2 January 2025: the petition was granted in full; Article 222 contradicts the 1945 Constitution and has no binding legal force (there was a dissenting opinion) — [Kontan](https://nasional.kontan.co.id/news/mahkamah-konstitusi-mk-hapus-ketentuan-presidential-threshold); [Bisnis](https://kabar24.bisnis.com/read/20250102/15/1828505/sekjen-golkar-kaget-mk-tiba-tiba-hapus-presidential-threshold-20); [Hukumonline: Commission II will revise the Election Law](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/komisi-ii-dpr-bakal-revisi-uu-pemilu-sesuai-putusan-mk-lt6777f20d7e4ee/)
- The MK also gave "constitutional engineering" guidelines to the lawmakers (so that the number of pairs is not too large, parties that do not nominate are sanctioned by being barred from the next election, etc.) — discussed in [Kontan: full considerations](https://nasional.kontan.co.id/news/mk-hapus-ketentuan-presidential-threshold-ini-pertimbangan-lengkapnya) (details of the points need checking in the text of the ruling)

**Age requirement for presidential candidates**
- Article 169 letter q of Law 7/2017 (minimum age 40) was reinterpreted by MK Decision 90/PUU-XXI/2023 (16 Oct 2023): "at least 40 years old or have held/currently hold an office chosen through general elections including regional head elections"; MK Chief Justice Anwar Usman was subsequently removed as Chief Justice by the MK Honour Council (MKMK) for ethics violations related to this ruling — [Hukumonline: MK rulings 2023](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/putusan-mk-dan-peristiwa-yang-menarik-perhatian-publik-sepanjang-2023-lt6596ea11a9d0f?page=all); [DPR Info Singkat May 2024](https://berkas.dpr.go.id/pusaka/files/info_singkat/Info%20Singkat-XVI-9-I-P3DI-Mei-2024-187.pdf)

**DPR / DPRD legislative elections**
- DPR: 575 seats in 80 electoral districts (Law 7/2017), increased to 580 seats / 84 districts through Perppu 1/2022 (12 Dec 2022, became Law 7/2023) for the new Papua provinces; districts of 3–10 seats — [Wikipedia: Indonesian electoral law of 2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_electoral_law_of_2017)
- Open-list proportional system: voters can mark a candidate; party seats are computed with the Webster/Sainte-Laguë method after parties below the threshold are removed; seats within a party go to the candidates with the most votes — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_electoral_law_of_2017)
- Parliamentary threshold of 4% of valid national votes (Article 414 paragraph 1), only for the DPR, not for the DPRD — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_electoral_law_of_2017); [Tirto](https://tirto.id/apa-saja-syarat-parpol-agar-lolos-parliamentary-threshold-gVXy)
- The MK (Decision 116/PUU-XXI/2023, Feb 2024) declared Article 414 paragraph (1) conditionally constitutional: it still applies to the 2024 DPR election, and does not apply to the next election unless changed by the lawmakers — [Detik](https://news.detik.com/pemilu/d-7219132/pd-dorong-ambang-batas-capres-dihapus-usai-mk-putuskan-pt-4-harus-diubah); [DPR Info Singkat](https://berkas.dpr.go.id/pusaka/files/info_singkat/Info%20Singkat-XVI-9-I-P3DI-Mei-2024-187.pdf). In January 2026 there was a new lawsuit against Article 414 paragraph 1 (status unknown) — [Katadata](https://katadata.co.id/indepth/telaah/6abce48d97b00/pertarungan-ambang-batas-parlemen-kepentingan-partai-dan-nasib-psi-pada-2029)
- Provincial DPRD 35–120 seats according to population (exceptions Aceh 81, Jakarta 106); regency/city DPRD 20–55 seats — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_electoral_law_of_2017)
- Campaign donation limits: individuals Rp2.5 billion, business entities Rp25 billion — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_electoral_law_of_2017)

**Pilkada (governors, regents, mayors)**
- Article 18 paragraph 4 UUD: regional heads are "elected democratically" (not explicitly "directly") — [UUD 1945](https://id.wikisource.org/wiki/Undang-Undang_Dasar_Negara_Republik_Indonesia_Tahun_1945). This is the basis of the argument that a pilkada through the DPRD is not unconstitutional (statement by the Coordinating Minister for Law, Human Rights, Immigration and Corrections (Menko Kumham Imipas), Jan 2026) — [Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/menko-kumham-imipas--pilkada-lewat-dprd-tak-bertentangan-dengan-konstitusi-lt6965cf5e5d9cc?page=3)
- MK Decision 60/PUU-XXII/2024 (20 Aug 2024): the nomination threshold for regional heads by parties is no longer 20% of DPRD seats/25% of votes, but a percentage of valid votes based on the regional permanent voter list (DPT), ranging from 6.5%–10% (examples: DPT >1 million → 6.5%; province with 6–12 million residents → 7.5%); Article 40 paragraph (3) of the Pilkada Law was annulled, paragraph (1) conditionally unconstitutional; effective immediately for Pilkada 2024 — [Antara](https://babel.antaranews.com/berita/426875/mk-ubah-ambang-batas-pencalonan-calon-kepala-daerah); [CNN Indonesia (Perludem)](https://www.cnnindonesia.com/nasional/20240820125947-617-1135246/perludem-putusan-mk-soal-syarat-pencalonan-berlaku-untuk-pilkada-2024/amp)
- MK Decision 70/PUU-XXII/2024 (20 Aug 2024): the minimum age of regional-head candidates is counted at the time the KPU (General Elections Commission) confirms the candidate pair — [NU Online](https://nu.or.id/nasional/putusan-mk-soal-perubahan-ambang-batas-pencalonan-pilkada-2024-pMHHW)
- The DPR briefly tried to circumvent the MK ruling on pilkada through the Legislative Body (Baleg) (Aug 2024) before it was cancelled — [Jakarta Post](https://www.thejakartapost.com/id/indonesia/2024/08/22/house-subverts-constitutional-court-on-regional-election-rules)
- Talk of pilkada through the DPRD: in Jan 2026 DPR Deputy Speaker Dasco stated there were no plans yet; revision of the Pilkada Law is not in the 2026 Priority National Legislation Programme (Prolegnas); the DPR prioritises revising Law 7/2017 (Elections) first — [CNBC Indonesia](https://www.cnbcindonesia.com/news/20260119113845-4-703308/dasco-belum-ada-rencana-pilkada-melalui-dprd); [Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/ini-penjelasan-resmi-dpr-pemerintah-terkait-pemilihan-kepala-daerah-melalui-dprd-lt696e0bcf684c1); [Antara](https://megapolitan.antaranews.com/berita/485898/dpr-dan-pemerintah-sepakat-tak-ada-revisi-uu-pilkada-tahun-ini)

**Separation of national vs local elections**
- MK Decision 135/PUU-XXII/2024 (read on 26 June 2025, petitioner Perludem): national elections (president, DPR, DPD) are separated from local elections (DPRD + regional heads); local elections are held at the earliest 2 years and at the latest 2.5 years after the inauguration of the national election results — [Kompas](https://nasional.kompas.com/read/2025/06/26/18192421/mk-soal-jeda-pemilu-nasional-dan-daerah-minimal-2-tahun-maksimal-25-tahun); [NU Online](https://nu.or.id/nasional/mk-putuskan-pemilu-dan-pilkada-dipisah-perludem-segera-revisi-undang-undangnya-ogPVl)
- Implications: national election in 2029, local election around 2031–2032; the terms of DPRD members elected in 2024 must be rearranged; the ruling was criticised for violating Article 22E(1) "once every five years" — [Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/krisis-konstitusionalitas-pemisahan-pemilu-nasional-dan-daerah-lt688d937ad5ab1/?page=all); [Antara Jatim](https://jatim.antaranews.com/berita/945149/solusi-untuk-buah-simalakama-putusan-mk-nomor-135-puu-xxii-2024)
- July 2026: deliberation of the Pilkada Bill is likely only after the Elections Law revision is finished — [Metro TV](https://www.metrotvnews.com/read/KvJCJO20-dpr-tidak-ada-agenda-pembahasan-revisi-uu-pilkada-di-prolegnas-2026)

### Inferences
- Presidential simulation rule: `candidates = pairs nominated by ≥1 party participating in the election` (no threshold, 2025 version), `win if share>50% && provinces_with_≥20% > N_prov/2 else runoff(top2)`, `term=5, max_terms=2`, the president cannot dissolve parliament.
- DPR rule: multi-seat districts of 3–10, open list, Sainte-Laguë (divisors 1,3,5,…), parties <4% nationally eliminated (a changeable parameter, since the 4% was declared to need revision for 2029).
- Two pilkada routes that can serve as a simulation "switch": direct (current default, threshold 6.5–10% of votes based on the DPT) vs through the DPRD (a proposal, constitutional according to the government).
- Calendar: national election in year T, local election at T+2 to T+2.5.
- The "MK" actor in the simulation is very powerful: it can change election rules at any time through judicial review (presidential threshold, pilkada threshold, candidate age, election schedule).

### Gaps
- The primary source of Law 7/2017 on the DPD (Regional Representatives Council) (from general knowledge: 4 members per province, non-party individual candidates, most-votes system) was not verified in this session.
- Other presidential candidate requirements in Article 169 (minimum high-school education, Indonesian citizen since birth, never sentenced to ≥5 years, etc.) were not verified from the sources fetched.
- The 30% female candidate quota, voting age 17 years/already married, campaign length (75 days in the 2024 election) — from general knowledge, not verified.
- The individual (independent) candidate route in pilkada with ID-card support requirements — not verified.
- The status of the Elections Law revision as of Nov 2026 (whether it has been passed, the new threshold figure) was not found.

## 2. Election management bodies: KPU, Bawaslu, DKPP, MK

### Takeaway
The Constitution requires the election organiser to be "national, permanent, and independent". KPU and Bawaslu (Election Supervisory Body) members are chosen in two stages: a selection committee formed by the President screens 2× the number of seats, then Commission II of the DPR tests (fit and proper) and chooses. The MK (9 judges, 3 nominated by the President, 3 by the DPR, 3 by the Supreme Court (MA)) decides election result disputes with finality.

### Cited Findings
- Article 22E paragraph 5 UUD: "General elections are organised by a general elections commission that is national, permanent, and independent." — [UUD 1945](https://id.wikisource.org/wiki/Undang-Undang_Dasar_Negara_Republik_Indonesia_Tahun_1945)
- 2022–2027 selection: the selection team submitted 14 KPU candidate names and 10 Bawaslu candidate names to the President; the President forwarded them to the DPR; the DPR had 30 days to choose 7 KPU members and 5 Bawaslu members from 24 names; Commission II held the fit and proper test on 14–16 Feb 2022, then it was ratified in a plenary meeting — [Bisnis](https://kabar24.bisnis.com/read/20220214/15/1500008/fit-and-proper-test-calon-anggota-kpu-bawaslu-dimulai-hari-ini); [Katadata](https://katadata.co.id/berita/nasional/6200e16584915/komisi-ii-dpr-bersiap-seleksi-24-calon-anggota-kpu-dan-bawaslu); [Detik: mechanism of the fitness test](https://news.detik.com/berita/d-3461051/begini-mekanisme-uji-kelayakan-calon-anggota-kpu-bawaslu-di-dpr)
- Academic criticism: the DPR's fit and proper test makes the selection of election referees prone to party interests (meritocracy vs politics) — [Jurnal Unnes JPI](https://journal.unnes.ac.id/journals/jpi/article/view/6293)
- MK: 9 constitutional judges are confirmed by the President, with 3 each nominated by the MA, the DPR, and the President; it has authority to adjudicate at first and final instance with a final ruling for judicial review of laws, disputes over the authority of state institutions, dissolution of parties, and disputes over election results; it must rule on the DPR's opinion regarding presidential violations — [UUD 1945 Article 24C](https://id.wikisource.org/wiki/Undang-Undang_Dasar_Negara_Republik_Indonesia_Tahun_1945)
- The MK itself is supervised by the MK Honour Council (MKMK), which in 2023 removed Anwar Usman from the Chief Justice seat for ethics violations — [Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/putusan-mk-dan-peristiwa-yang-menarik-perhatian-publik-sepanjang-2023-lt6596ea11a9d0f?page=all)

### Inferences
- The "selection committee (pansel) → shortlist of 2× seats → DPR chooses" pattern can be used as a generic template for filling independent bodies in the simulation (KPU, Bawaslu, KPK, KY (Judicial Commission)).
- A clear separation of functions for agents: KPU = implements (voter roll, candidate verification, vote counting); Bawaslu = supervises & acts on administrative violations; DKPP (Election Organiser Ethics Council) = ethics trials of organisers (can dismiss KPU/Bawaslu members); MK = result disputes (final).

### Gaps
- The composition of the DKPP (from general knowledge: 7 people — 1 KPU element, 1 Bawaslu element, 5 public figures: 3 nominated by the DPR, 2 by the President) was not verified in this session.
- The KPU/Bawaslu 5-year term and the number of provincial/regency KPU members were not verified from primary sources.
- The MK's deadline for deciding presidential election disputes (14 working days) and legislative ones (30 working days) — from general knowledge, not verified.
- The selection of KPU/Bawaslu for the 2027–2032 period has not appeared in search results.

## 3. Voting = a right, not a duty; participation rates

### Takeaway
There is no sanction for citizens who do not vote (golput); participation in the 2024 elections was about 81–82% for the presidential/legislative elections, but the simultaneous Nov 2024 pilkada was only about 68–71%.

### Cited Findings
- Participation in the 2024 presidential election: 81.78% (KPU), slightly below the 2019 presidential election's 81.97%; DPR legislative election 81.42%; DPD 81.36%; exceeding the RPJMN (National Medium-Term Development Plan) target of 79.5% — [IDX Channel](https://www.idxchannel.com/news/tembus-81-persen-wamendagri-apresiasi-tingkat-partisipasi-pemilih-pemilu-2024/all); [Bisnis](https://kabar24.bisnis.com/read/20240325/15/1752488/pemilu-2024-partisipasi-pemilih-turun-golput-naik)
- Participation in the simultaneous pilkada of 27 Nov 2024: national average ±68% according to KPU Chair Mochammad Afifuddin — [Antara](https://www.antaranews.com/berita/4511329/kpu-ri-luar-biasa-partisipasi-pemilih-pilkada-capai-68-persen); [Tirto](https://tirto.id/kpu-sebut-partisipasi-pemilih-di-pilkada-2024-hanya-68-persen-g6sn); differs from the 71% figure at [Kumparan](https://kumparan.com/kumparannews/ketua-kpu-partisipasi-pemilih-pilkada-2024-rata-rata-71-248p0B6meD8) (the official final figure was not found)
- Jakarta pilkada 2024 participation ±58% — [Bisnis](https://kabar24.bisnis.com/read/20241129/15/1820334/kpu-ungkap-partisipasi-pemilih-di-pilkada-2024-ada-di-bawah-70-persen)

### Inferences
- Simulation: voting is optional; realistic turnout parameters ±82% for national elections and ±68% for local elections; local turnout falls when local elections draw less attention (a reason cited by the KPU).

### Gaps
- The explicit legal basis for "voting is a right" (Law 7/2017 uses the phrase "has the right to vote"; there is no sanction for golput, only criminal penalties for those who obstruct people from voting or urge golput in exchange for rewards) was not verified from the primary text in this session.

## 4. Appointment of the National Police Chief (Kapolri) and TNI Commander (Panglima TNI) (President proposes → DPR approves); the role of Kompolnas

### Takeaway
For both offices: the President chooses one candidate (a prerogative right), sends a letter to the DPR, the relevant commission (Commission III for Kapolri, Commission I for Panglima) holds a fit and proper test, and the DPR plenary approves/rejects within 20 days. Kapolri: DPR silence for 20 days = deemed approval; the candidate must be an active Polri senior officer. Panglima: the candidate = an active senior officer who is or has been a service chief of staff, "may" rotate between services; if the DPR refuses, the President must submit another name.

### Cited Findings
**Kapolri (Law 2/2002 Article 11)**
- The Kapolri is appointed and dismissed by the President with DPR approval; the proposal is submitted by the President to the DPR together with reasons; the DPR must approve or reject no later than 20 days after the President's letter is received; if the DPR gives no answer, the candidate is deemed approved; in urgent circumstances the President may temporarily dismiss the Kapolri and appoint an Acting Kapolri (Plt) and then ask for DPR approval; the candidate is an active Polri senior officer, taking rank progression and career into account — [Hukumonline: procedure for appointing Kapolri](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/melihat-tata-cara-pengangkatan-kapolri-lt5ff577b69876c?page=all)
- Elucidation of the article: the proposal is the President's prerogative right; DPR approval serves as a checks-and-balances mechanism — [Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/melihat-tata-cara-pengangkatan-kapolri-lt5ff577b69876c)
- Kompolnas (National Police Commission) (Presidential Regulation 17/2011 Article 6 paragraphs 1–2) gives the President considerations on candidates for Kapolri (senior officers); the final decision stays with the President; practical order: Kompolnas proposes names → the President chooses → Commission III fit and proper → DPR plenary — [Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/melihat-tata-cara-pengangkatan-kapolri-lt5ff577b69876c?page=all)
- Example: in 2021 the President sent a presidential letter (surpres) with the single candidate Komjen Listyo Sigit Prabowo — [Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/dpr-terima-surpres--komjen-listyo-sigit-calon-tunggal-kapolri-lt5ffe99dc44477?page=all)
- Wanjakti is not mandatory for the nomination of the Kapolri (statement by the Coordinating Minister for Political, Legal and Security Affairs, 2015) — [CNN Indonesia](https://www.cnnindonesia.com/politik/20150212192755-32-31751/tedjo-pencalonan-kapolri-tak-harus-melalui-seleksi-wanjakti/)
- Judicial review of Article 11: the MK rejected the argument of unconstitutionality of Article 11 paragraph (1); on 16 April 2026 another petition against Article 11 was declared inadmissible — [MKRI](https://www.mkri.id/berita/-23090); [MKRI case summary 77/2026](https://s.mkri.id/public/content/persidangan/sinopsis/ikhtisar_4919_2963_ikhtisar%2077_2026%20(sudah%20koreksi).pdf)
- There is no fixed term for the Kapolri; replacement is described as the President's prerogative right (Bisnis, 30 Aug 2025) — [Bisnis](https://kabar24.bisnis.com/read/20250830/16/1907099/penggantian-kapolri-jadi-hak-prerogatif-presiden-ini-dasar-hukumnya)

**TNI Commander (Panglima TNI) (Law 34/2004 Article 13)**
- The President appoints and dismisses the Panglima after obtaining DPR approval (paragraph 2) — [Kemhan Itjen](https://www.kemhan.go.id/itjen/?p=6896)
- The Panglima post may be held in rotation by active senior officers from each service who are or have been service chiefs of staff (paragraph 4) — [Hukumonline: rules for replacing the Panglima](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/aturan-pergantian-panglima-tni-di-indonesia-lt637f4e4aaa9ac?page=all); [RCTI+](https://www.rctiplus.com/news/detail/inews/5494626/ksad-jenderal-tandyo-respons-isu-jadi-calon-panglima-tni--domain-presiden)
- DPR approval is delivered no later than 20 days, excluding recess, from receipt of the request (paragraph 6) — [Antara Jatim](https://jatim.antaranews.com/berita/659821/dpr-ri-segera-proses-pergantian-panglima-tni-sebelum-masa-reses)
- If the DPR refuses, the President proposes one other candidate (paragraph 7); the DPR must give written reasons if it refuses — [Kompas TV](https://www.kompas.tv/article/349510/mekanisme-pergantian-panglima-tni-menunggu-penerus-jenderal-andika-perkasa); [Antara Gorontalo](https://gorontalo.antaranews.com/berita/214149/wapres-maruf-presiden-akan-segera-umumkan-nama-panglima-tni)
- The examination process in DPR Commission I, e.g. 2017 single candidate Air Marshal Hadi Tjahjanto — [Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/marsekal-hadi-tjahjanto-calon-tunggal-panglima-tni-lt5a24f9e54d8dd); study of the 2022 replacement process — [DPR Info Singkat Dec 2022](https://berkas.dpr.go.id/pusaka/files/info_singkat/Info%20Singkat-XIV-23-I-P3DI-Desember-2022-1947.pdf)
- Rotation between services is "may" (not mandatory); the selection of Andika (Army) was debated over the services' turn — [Medcom](https://www.medcom.id/nasional/politik/GNlgE6PK-di-balik-pemilihan-andika-sebagai-panglima-tni-tak-perlu-berdrama-soal-matra)

The working committee (Panja) still recommends that the Kapolri be appointed/dismissed by the President with DPR approval (MPR Decrees VI & VII/2000) — [Hukumonline: 7 substantive points](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/babak-baru-ruu-polri--ini-7-substansi-perubahan-lt6a14202a63f7e?page=all); [Antara](https://www.antaranews.com/berita/5574708/rapat-paripurna-setujui-ruu-polri-jadi-usul-inisiatif-dpr)

### Inferences
- Kapolri simulation rule: `nominate(president, candidate ∈ active_police_generals)` → `committee_hearing(Commission III)` → `plenary_vote` within 20 days; `silence ⇒ approved`; `emergency ⇒ acting chief then seek approval`; no fixed term (can be replaced at any time by the President with DPR approval). Kompolnas = shortlist advisor, no veto.
- Panglima rule: `candidate ∈ {current/former chiefs of staff Army/Navy/Air Force}`, service rotation preference (soft rule), 20 days excluding recess, `rejected ⇒ president must submit a different name` (no confirmed "deemed approved").
- A design that can be simplified: a single candidate per proposal (common practice), not a list.

## 5. Internal police & military careers: recruitment, ranks, promotion board, who appoints Kapolda/Kapolres

### Takeaway
Polri has 4 entry points (Akpol & SIPSS → officer Ipda; Bintara → Bripda; Tamtama → Bharada). Promotion to senior posts/ranks is decided internally through a Wanjakti session (Policy Council for Senior Ranks and Posts) chaired by the Kapolri, then executed through a Kapolri Telegram Letter (Surat Telegram, ST) — Kapolda (regional police chiefs) and Kapolres (district police chiefs) are appointed/transferred by the Kapolri, not the President or the DPR.

### Cited Findings
- Four Polri recruitment tracks: Akpol Cadet, SIPSS (Police Inspector School for Graduate Sources), Bintara, Tamtama; Tamtama is for men only — [Tirto](https://tirto.id/beda-taruna-akpol-bintara-polri-dan-tamtama-gXUK); [Kompas TV](https://www.kompas.tv/ekonomi/571821/pendaftaran-anggota-polri-2025-segera-dibuka-cek-jalur-dan-persyaratannya)
- Akpol: 4 years of education, graduating as Police Inspector Second Class (Ipda); SIPSS: for graduates, ±6 months at Lemdiklat Semarang, graduating as Ipda; Bintara graduates as Bripda; Tamtama ±5 months, graduating as Bharada — [Brain Academy](https://www.brainacademy.id/blog/perbedaan-akpol-bintara-dan-tamtama); [SIPSS 2025 announcement, polri.go.id](https://penerimaan.polri.go.id/uploads/pdf/PENGUMUMAN_PENERIMAAN_SIPSS_T.A._2025.pdf)
- Wanjakti is chaired by the Kapolri; for Wakapolri (Deputy Kapolri), the candidates = three-star senior officers (Komjen); the final decision is the Kapolri's; assisted by the Career Consideration Council — [Kontan](https://nasional.kontan.co.id/news/wanjakti-segera-memilih-nama-wakapolri); [Bisnis](https://kabar24.bisnis.com/read/20150417/16/424030/siapa-wakapolri-wanjakti-segera-bersidang); [CNN Indonesia](https://www.cnnindonesia.com/politik/20150212192755-32-31751/tedjo-pencalonan-kapolri-tak-harus-melalui-seleksi-wanjakti/)
- Tiered Baperjakat (Rank and Post Consideration Board) for echelon II posts and below: the central level is formed by the Kapolri, then the head of the parent unit, Kapolda, and Kapolres — [peraturan.go.id BN 319/2010](https://peraturan.go.id/files/bn319-2010.pdf)
- Perpol 10/2025: the Kapolri's Assistant for Human Resources prepares post candidates through selection/competence tests, decided by a career consideration council session (text needs verification) — [Hukumonline klinik](https://www.hukumonline.com/klinik/a/apakah-polisi-dapat-menduduki-jabatan-sipil-lt694cb4104dcad/)
- The Kapolri issues telegram letters for the posts of Kapolda, senior officers, principal Polda officials, Kapolres; one ST can contain promotions, demotions, and retirements at once — [Kontan](https://nasional.kontan.co.id/news/kapolri-jenderal-listyo-sigit-mutasi-704-personel-polri); [Medcom: Sep 2024 transfers including demotions](https://www.medcom.id/nasional/peristiwa/Rb1DgWdN-daftar-mutasi-polri-september-2024-4-kapolda-baru-hingga-demosi)

- Scale: on 25 June 2026, 1,121 senior and mid-ranking officers were rotated through 7 STs; at the end of June 2026, 87 senior officers were promoted — [RCTI+/Okezone](https://www.rctiplus.com/news/detail/okezone/5415005/polri-mutasi-1-121-personel--ada-kapolda-hingga-wakapolda); [Merahputih](https://www.merahputih.com/post/read/kapolri-mutasi-3-kapolda)

- The extension of the TNI retirement age is criticised for causing career stagnation & a pile-up of officers — [NU Online](https://www.nu.or.id/nasional/perpanjangan-usia-pensiun-perwira-dinilai-picu-stagnasi-dan-diskriminasi-internal-tni-UFw6e)

### Inferences
- Simulation rule: pipeline `recruit(track) → rank_start` (officer: Ipda; bintara: Bripda; tamtama: Bharada); promotion of rank & senior posts = `board(chaired by chief) recommends → chief decides → telegram order`. ## 6. Checks & balances institutions: BPK, KPK, Ombudsman, Judicial Commission, presidential impeachment

### Takeaway
Three-stage impeachment: DPR (2/3 of votes in a meeting attended by 2/3 of members) → MK rules within 90 days → MPR (People's Consultative Assembly) convenes within 30 days and decides (quorum 3/4, 2/3 of those present agree). The BPK (Audit Board) is chosen by the DPR (with consideration from the DPD); Judicial Commission members are appointed by the President with DPR approval; KPK leaders are chosen by the DPR (5 of 10 pansel names), with a 5-year term and a maximum of 2 terms since MK 112/PUU-XX/2022.

### Cited Findings
- Article 7A UUD: the President/Vice President may be dismissed by the MPR on a proposal from the DPR if proven to have committed legal violations (treason, corruption, bribery, other serious crimes), disgraceful conduct, or no longer meeting the requirements — [UUD 1945](https://id.wikisource.org/wiki/Undang-Undang_Dasar_Negara_Republik_Indonesia_Tahun_1945)
- Article 7B: the DPR must first ask the MK to examine its opinion; submission to the MK needs support from ≥2/3 of DPR members present in a plenary meeting attended by ≥2/3 of members; the MK must rule within at most 90 days; if proven, the DPR forwards the proposal to the MPR; the MPR must convene within 30 days at the latest; the MPR decision is taken in a meeting attended by ≥3/4 of members and approved by ≥2/3 of those present, after the President/Vice President is given the opportunity to give an explanation — [UUD 1945](https://id.wikisource.org/wiki/Undang-Undang_Dasar_Negara_Republik_Indonesia_Tahun_1945)
- Article 23F: BPK members are chosen by the DPR taking into account the DPD's consideration and confirmed by the President; BPK leaders are chosen from and by the members — [UUD 1945](https://id.wikisource.org/wiki/Undang-Undang_Dasar_Negara_Republik_Indonesia_Tahun_1945)
- Article 24B: the Judicial Commission is independent, authorised to propose the appointment of supreme court justices and to safeguard the honour/behaviour of judges; its members are appointed & dismissed by the President with DPR approval — [UUD 1945](https://id.wikisource.org/wiki/Undang-Undang_Dasar_Negara_Republik_Indonesia_Tahun_1945)
- KPK (Corruption Eradication Commission): the President forms a pansel (government + community elements; in 2024 chaired by BPKP Head Yusuf Ateh, 9 people); the pansel submits 10 names to the President, and the DPR (Commission III) chooses 5 for the 2024–2029 period — [Antara Babel](https://babel.antaranews.com/berita/411267/istana-umumkan-9-nama-pansel-kpk-yang-dipimpin-kepala-bpkp-yusuf-ateh); [Bisnis](https://kabar24.bisnis.com/read/20240801/16/1787382/mensesneg-pratikno-klaim-jokowi-tak-cawe-cawe-seleksi-capim-kpk); [Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/soal-capim--pansel-kpk-rapat-dengan-komisi-iii-dpr-lt5d7628703a03c)
- MK Decision 112/PUU-XX/2022 (25 May 2023): the term of KPK leaders is interpreted as 5 years (formerly 4) and a maximum of 2 terms, also applying to leaders currently in office — [Bisnis](https://kabar24.bisnis.com/read/20230525/15/1659382/wapres-maruf-dukung-keputusan-mk-perpanjang-masa-jabatan-pimpinan-kpk); [Antara Ambon](https://ambon.antaranews.com/berita/167343/jubir-mk-putusan-langsung-berlaku-untuk-pimpinan-kpk-periode-ini)

### Inferences
- Impeachment simulation rule: `dpr_vote(quorum≥2/3, yes≥2/3)` → `court_review(≤90 days, guilty?)` → `mpr_vote(≤30 days, quorum≥3/4, yes≥2/3 present)`. The court acts as a legal filter, the final decision is political.
- Oversight-body selection templates: the President forms a pansel → 2× shortlist → DPR chooses (KPK); or the DPR chooses directly (BPK); or the President appoints + DPR approves (KY). These variations can be used to set how "independent" each body is in the simulation.

## 7. The ASN merit system for appointing officials

### Takeaway
Law 20/2023 on the State Civil Apparatus (ASN) (31 Oct 2023) abolished the KASN (State Civil Apparatus Commission) and delegated merit supervision to ministries/agencies; the MK (121/PUU-XXII/2024, 16 Oct 2025) declared that delegation article conditionally unconstitutional and ordered the formation of an independent body supervising the merit system within 2 years at most (± Oct 2027).

### Cited Findings
- Law 20/2023 on ASN was enacted on 31 October 2023 — [peraturan.bpk.go.id](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/269470/uu-no-20-)
- MK Decision 121/PUU-XXII/2024 (petitioners Perludem, KPPOD, ICW), read on 16 Oct 2025: Article 26 paragraph (2) letter d of Law 20/2023 is conditionally unconstitutional insofar as it is not interpreted as meaning that supervision of the merit system (including the principles, code of ethics, and code of conduct of ASN) is carried out by an independent body; "The independent body in question must be formed within at most 2 years from the date this ruling is pronounced." — [Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/uu-asn-inkonstitusional-bersyarat--mk-minta-dibentuk-lembaga-independen-lt68f0d7eb2a9e9/)
- Law 20/2023 opens the possibility for active TNI/Polri members to occupy certain ASN posts, criticised as threatening neutrality & merit — [Jurnal Taqnin UINSU](https://jurnal.uinsu.ac.id/index.php/taqnin/article/download/23775/10197)
- Open selection for Senior Leadership Positions (JPT) is criticised because the weighting can be subjective — [Jurnal Syntax Literate](https://jurnal.syntaxliterate.co.id/index.php/syntax-literate/article/view/61782); JPT Pratama practice in the regions — [JDIH Buton](https://jdih.butonkab.go.id/assets/file_konten/artikel/artikel_Penerapan-Sistem-Merit-dalam-Pengisian-Jabatan-Pimpinan-Tinggi-Pratama-di-Indonesia.pdf)

### Inferences
- Simulation rule for bureaucratic (non-political) officials: `open_selection(panel) → top3 → appointing_official (PPK: minister/regional head) picks one`, supervised by an independent merit body that can annul appointments that violate merit. A regional head elected in a pilkada = the PPK in their region → a weak point for politicisation of the bureaucracy (can be turned into a corruption/patronage mechanic in the game).

### Gaps
- The current JPT mechanism under Law 20/2023 (open selection, selection committee, top 3 candidates, the role of BKN & KemenPAN-RB) was not verified from the primary text.
- Whether the independent body replacing the KASN has been formed as of Nov 2026 — not found.
- The ban on regional heads transferring officials 6 months before/after a pilkada (Pilkada Law) — from general knowledge, not verified.
